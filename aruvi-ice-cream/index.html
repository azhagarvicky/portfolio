Your neighbourhood stop for something cool

Aruvi is a friendly little shop for ice creams and everyday dairy. Stop by for an Arun ice cream with the family, or pick up Arokya milk and Hatsun curd, paneer and ghee for the kitchen on your way home.

Alongside the freezers, we also take enquiries for Aari embroidery and saree pre-pleating, so you can plan an outfit for a special day while you're here.
